Leonida Trophy Guide

Grand Theft Auto VI · PlayStation 5 · 55 trophies · offline

0/550% to platinum

⚠ Only 1 trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — do Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends.

    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no online trophies — PowerPyx. The story takes 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist takes about 100 more. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Guides report that cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off for the whole run.

    The 100% checklist — what A Long Road actually needs

    The checklist is closer to 60% true completion, per PowerPyx — you do not need everything. The thresholds are: 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned · 100 of 255 animals studied through the binoculars · 70 of 80 challenges · 300 stunts · 15 meditation spots on the checklist (the trophy counts 18) · 50 of 66 weapons. Earlier progress counts, and nothing on the checklist is missable.

    Missable watch list

    PowerPyx flags only one trophy as possibly missable. Seven more have triggers no guide has pinned down yet, so they sit on this watch list until the guides do — they are not known to be missable, but they are the ones to check again after launch. Everything else in the list cannot be missed: story trophies pop when you finish their mission, and free-roam after the story keeps every side trophy reachable. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

    About & sources

    An unofficial fan guide, written for this entry in our own words. The trophy names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them; every guide names where its facts come from, beside the trophy. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshot is used — the look is our own.

    Sources

      How the list was checked

      The 55 names, descriptions and grades were taken from the official list as published on PlayStation Network (Exophase / PSNProfiles, 18 November 2026): 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 27 visible, 28 hidden until earned. Counts, the roadmap and the missable flag come from PowerPyx, cross-checked against GTABase, OutputLag, CryptoGames and GTA-Zone. Where the guides disagree — the meditation count is 18 spots for the trophy and 15 on the 100% checklist — this guide reports both numbers instead of picking one. Seven trophies are marked unconfirmed: the guides have not pinned down their exact trigger yet, so their guides say what the description plainly reads and say so.
